Swach Farm - Homepage Gallery Carousel
=======================================

The "Farm Gallery" carousel on the homepage (public/index.html) looks for
six photos in this folder, named exactly:

  farm-1.jpg
  farm-2.jpg
  farm-3.jpg
  farm-4.jpg
  farm-5.jpg
  farm-6.jpg

To show your own real-time farm photos, just save your images with those
exact filenames into this folder (public/assets/gallery/) and re-deploy
the site. No code changes are needed - the carousel picks them up
automatically.

Until a photo is added, that slide shows a branded color placeholder with
an icon and caption instead of a broken image, so the site always looks
finished.

Suggested photo order (captions already written to match):
  farm-1.jpg -> Our Herd at Pasture
  farm-2.jpg -> Fresh Morning Milking
  farm-3.jpg -> Open Natural Pastures
  farm-4.jpg -> Traditional Ghee Making
  farm-5.jpg -> Fresh Paneer, Made Daily
  farm-6.jpg -> Life at Swach Farm

Tip: for fast loading, resize photos to roughly 1600x900px and keep each
file under 500KB before uploading.
