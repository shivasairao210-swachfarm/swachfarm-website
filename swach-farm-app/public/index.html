Our Story

Rooted in Revelly Village, Karimnagar

A small family farm with a simple promise: milk exactly as nature intended, delivered with care to every family in the region.

Swach Farm

Village of Revelly, Karimnagar District, Telangana, India

Swach Farm began with a single goal — to bring truly pure, unadulterated milk back into everyday life. Our herd grazes freely on natural pasture in Revelly village, and every drop of milk we sell is free from preservatives, chemicals, and synthetic additives.

We believe fresh milk is more than a product — it is the foundation of a healthy family. That is why we deliver daily around Karimnagar and welcome pickup straight from our farm gate, so you always know exactly where your milk comes from.

Can't make it to the farm? No problem. Our doorstep delivery service brings the same farm-fresh milk, cheese and butter to homes across Karimnagar town and surrounding areas — chilled, timely and reliable.

  • ✅ Daily doorstep delivery across Karimnagar and nearby villages
